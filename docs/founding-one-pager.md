# TrustMCP — founding members

**Trust, made machine-readable. Publish once. Assess on your own terms.**

Third-party risk runs on a broken loop: every customer emails every vendor the same
questionnaire; vendors re-answer forever; the answers are stale the day they're filed.
The **TrustMCP** flips it from a *request* model to a *publish*
model — and makes the evidence readable by the assessment agents both sides are now
deploying.

## How it works

- A vendor **publishes once**: SOC 2, pentest, ISO, COI, SBOM, DPA — plus
  machine-readable claims (`mfa.enforced`, `data_residency`, `breach_notification_hours`).
- The vendor stays in control: customers **request scoped, revocable access**; every
  read is logged. Public docs are open; sensitive docs require approval.
- A customer (or its agent, over MCP) reads the profile in a handful of calls and
  **computes its own verdict** against its own framework.
- The network is a **thin trust anchor** — it verifies domain ownership and issues the
  **`agent-ready`** mark. It never scores or rates anyone.

## Why now

Pooled-assessment networks shipped **stale, one-size verdicts**. TrustMCP shares the
**raw, current evidence** and leaves the judgment with each customer. And agents finally make machine-readable assurance worth publishing.

## Why it's safe to join

- **Open standard, Apache-2.0** — not a product of any one company; any tool can read it.
- **Free to publish and to consume.** No lock-in: profiles are plain JSON behind a
  documented API; vendors can move between operators.
- **You keep your standard.** TrustMCP standardizes *access*, never the verdict.
- **Low-barrier verified**, not a gatekeeper: the only thing the network guarantees is
  *who* is publishing (verified domain) — you verify the *content* yourself.

## What we're asking founding members to do

**Vendors (publishers)** — stand up a trust center (≈ an afternoon if you already run
Vanta/Drata/SafeBase), verify your domain, and grant access to a few design-partner
customers. Get the first `agent-ready` marks and a stronger story: *"we joined leading
brands to change how vendor risk is done."*

**Customers / GRC & TPRM teams (consumers)** — point your assessment process (or agent)
at TrustMCP for your design-partner vendors and give feedback on the claim taxonomy.

## The founding cohort

We're assembling a balanced group on both sides from people we already work with. If
you operate a trust portal today, publishing to TrustMCP is incremental and immediately
useful. Early members shape v0.1 → v1.0.
