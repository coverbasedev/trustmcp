# Membership & Participation (DRAFT)

> **Status: non-binding draft.** Dollar figures are placeholders for discussion.

TrustMCP is **free to use**. Membership classes exist to fund stewardship and to govern
the standard — not to gate access to evidence.

| Class | Who | Cost | What you get | Obligations |
|-------|-----|------|--------------|-------------|
| **Publisher** | Vendors publishing a profile | Free | Trust center hosting, the `agent-ready` mark after domain verification, scoped/revocable access control, audit log | Accurate identity; lawful content |
| **Consumer** | Customers & GRC/TPRM tools reading profiles | Free | Read profiles via REST/MCP; request scoped keys | Respect scope; don't redistribute another vendor's evidence |
| **Steward** | Organizations funding & governing TrustMCP | `[$X/yr by size]` | TSC/Board participation, roadmap influence, logo placement | Antitrust compliance; code of conduct |
| **Operator** | Entities running an accredited network node | `[cost-recovery]` | Right to operate a conformant node | Pass conformance tests; security audit; uptime + audit-log retention SLOs |

## Why "low-barrier verified" (not fully open, not gatekept)

- **Fully open** (anyone self-issues the mark) gives no trust floor — exactly the
  "sign any BS" problem.
- **Strict gatekeeping** (manual vetting, paid membership to publish) reproduces the
  friction TrustMCP is trying to remove and slows the founding group.
- **Low-barrier verified** keeps publishing free and instant, while the network
  guarantees the one thing software can cheaply verify: *who* is publishing.

## Founding cohort

We are recruiting a balanced founding group:

- **Publishers** that already run a trust center (Vanta / Drata / SafeBase
  customers) — publishing to TrustMCP is incremental and gives a stronger story.
- **Consumers** — GRC/TPRM teams and tools that want to read evidence directly with
  agents instead of sending questionnaires.

Founding members shape v0.1 → v1.0 and get the first `agent-ready` marks.
